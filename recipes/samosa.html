Samosa Recipe

Description

This is a delicious and crispy samosa recipe that's perfect for appetizers or snacks. The flaky pastry is filled with spiced minced meat.

Ingredients

Filling

  • 500 g minced meat
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Small handful of fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Samosa Wrappers

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup ghee or butter
  • 1/2 cup water

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, ghee, and water to make a stiff dough. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds. Once they splutter, add the ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Add the minced meat, ginger-garlic paste, and all the spices. Mix well and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Roll out the dough into small circles and cut them in half to make crescents.
  5. Fill each crescent with the meat mixture and seal the edges.
  6. Fry the samosas in hot oil until golden brown.
